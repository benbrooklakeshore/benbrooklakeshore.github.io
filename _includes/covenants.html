Covenants

Deed restrictions recorded for Benbrook Lakeshore Estates.

Restrictive covenants for Benbrook Lakeshore Estates were executed on January 27, 1986, and filed on January 28, 1986, in the Tarrant County Deed Records, Volume 8440, pages 1977–1981.

They replace the original 1953 developer restrictions in the plat (Volume 388-V, Page 55), as amended in Volume 2584, Page 575; Volume 3314, Page 227; and Volume 4130, Page 153. They renew automatically every 10 years unless the owners of 51% of the lots record a change.

View or download the covenants (PDF)

What the document covers

  • Architectural committee approval of plans
  • Setbacks
  • Minimum floor area and masonry, by area
  • Livestock
  • Signs
  • Septic
  • Oil and gas drilling limits
